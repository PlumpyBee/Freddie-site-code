About me

Hey, I’m Freddie.

I am a UX/UI designer who believes that strategic thinking, creativity and problem solving are all key elements that create enjoyable and delightful user centred experiences.

With my background in music and streaming I developed vital skills in live mediation, mentoring and motivational speaking. In addition to this, for the last 8 years I’ve been beekeeping, where patience and attention to detail have helped me build my own apiary.

The scenic route to design.

Music & streaming

I learned to read a room. Live mediation, mentoring and public speaking taught me how people think, listen and react.

Beekeeping

Eight years of keeping bees taught me patience, observation and attention to detail, and I built my own apiary along the way.

UX/UI design

Now I bring all of it to design: listening first, then shaping simple, human experiences.

Design with people at the centre.

Understand

I start by talking to people and digging into the problem, so I know what really needs solving before I design anything.

Design

I turn what I learn into wireframes and prototypes that are simple to use and easy to follow.

Refine

I test with real users, fix what is confusing, and keep improving until it is clear, simple and accessible.
